WASHINGTON, DECEMBER 1941

America enters a world war after the attack on Pearl Harbor.

A Nation at War

President Franklin D. Roosevelt needs help. The military needs ships, planes, food, fuel, and weapons. Factories must change what they make. Workers must fill new jobs. Families must deal with shortages.

You are part of the President's home-front organizing team. You will face ten real problems from World War II.

YOUR JOB
  • Read each news story.
  • Check the facts in the sidebar.
  • Compare four possible plans.
  • Pick a plan and lock it in.
  • Read the historical reveal and earn points.

Every plan has a pro and a con. Some plans help in one way but cause trouble in another. Think before you choose.

THE WAR AT A GLANCE

  • December 7, 1941: Japan attacks Pearl Harbor.
  • December 8: The United States declares war on Japan.
  • January 1942: The War Production Board is created.
  • The challenge: Turn America's factories and resources toward war.
