Top Secret • Presidential Assignment

Your Mission: Organize America's Home Front

Washington, December 1941. The attack on Pearl Harbor has brought the United States into World War II. President Franklin D. Roosevelt needs a team to coordinate factories, workers, food, money, public information, and civilian security.

You are a member of the President's home-front organizing team. Your job is to make ten difficult decisions that help mobilize the country for war.

Every option has advantages, costs, and consequences. There are no magical solutions: your team must balance speed, fairness, production, public cooperation, and the needs of American service members.

How to play

  • Read the historical intelligence briefing and inspect the photograph.
  • Choose one of four realistic policy decisions.
  • Read the consequences and track your team's national readiness.
  • Complete all ten challenges and submit a final presidential briefing.

This is a classroom simulation. Numerical changes are illustrative game mechanics, not actual historical measurements.
